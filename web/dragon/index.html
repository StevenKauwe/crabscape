  • Pick a mission on the quest map. Each one is a journey: fly from stop to stop, win the fight at each one, and watch the route along the bottom. Touching a monster only bumps you; watch for the red ring, that means it's about to strike! Gems you grab are banked when a stop is cleared. Out of hearts means you're retired until the mission ends, and if everyone is out, the mission is failed. You can try again from the stop you reached.
  • On an iPad: put a thumb down anywhere on the sky and slide it to steer. Hold the big Fire button to breathe fire.
  • Some missions give dragon eggs. Get 5 times tables right in a row to hatch one. One wrong answer freezes you for a minute!
  • Spend gems at the Roost to fix what the monsters broke, and watch Valmora heal.
  • Your gems and progress stay on this device. Spend them at the Roost.
  • Fly the same mission at the same time and your hits count together!
