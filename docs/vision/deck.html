CrabScape · 04
04The crab

Small. Heavy. Glowing.

  • Ten legs: two claws, six walking legs, two swimming paddles.
  • Two claws: a heavy crusher and a quick cutter.
  • A lantern of glowing yellow bacteria. A real strain glows yellow.
  • Found-litter armour: bottle-cap helmet, sea-glass shield.
  • Crabs walk sideways, so in a side-scroller you always see its face.
CrabScape · 05
05How it plays

A heavy-hitting tank.

Momentum. Power.
Timing. Anticipation.

Sink, push off the floor, paddle-stroke double jump. Sideways scuttle dodge. Crushing claw combos with real wind-ups. Timed parries, then the counter.

Enemy tellsare the animals’ real behaviour: a mantis shrimp cocking its club.
CrabScape · 06
The loop · one zone deeper each time
forage→prepare→craft→build→traverse

Depth is wealth: the rarest things wait in the deepest, deadliest places.
Die, and you keep the gear you wore. What you carried waits in your cast-off shell where you fell. Dive back for it, or a friend does.
