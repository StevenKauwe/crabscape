# Vision

A cozy-but-brutal adventure, solo or co-op: you are a small crab exploring a vast, strange ocean. Forage, cook,
craft and build your way deeper, and come home alive.

## The hook
**Feels like RuneScape. Fights like Silksong. Comes home like Valheim. Underwater, and you're a crab.**

In the owner's words (2026-11-11): it should *feel* like RuneScape, with the motivations, the tasks and the reasons
to move about and explore; play like Silksong in combat; and have a strong home base between adventures that the
others in the world share, and that we build together, like Valheim.

- **Feels like RuneScape (the reason to play):** there is always a next thing you want. Skills that grow by use,
  tasks and quests with a clear "go here, get that", gathering feeding crafting, rarer things from deeper places,
  and places you can see but can't reach yet. Every region answers "why would I go there?" before "what does it
  look like?"
- **Fights like Silksong (the moment to moment):** skilful, readable, punishing combat and movement, with levels
  and gear that work like Elden Ring's: they *lower the skill a fight needs*, never remove it. An over-levelled,
  well-geared crab has more room for mistakes (more shell to lose, harder hits, wider timing windows); an
  under-levelled one can still win on skill. It's never "click and see if you win". (We're a heavy crab, not a
  needle: see `how-it-plays.md`.)
- **Comes home like Valheim (the place between adventures):** a strong home base that everyone in your world shares
  and builds together. You leave it to adventure, come back to it to rest, cook, craft and show off, and it grows
  with the world.
- **The twist:** every trip is a dive. Deeper means colder, darker, less oxygen, more pressure and stranger life,
  and you always have to plan the way back up.

## Every way to play pays
In the owner's words (2026-11-11): mining one thing for two hours while watching TV should be totally doable;
farming low-level pistol shrimp for loot should feel rewarding; and if you want to build, you should feel obliged
to take part in the activities that the core and rare materials come from.

- **Low-attention gathering is a real way to play.** A gathering spot near home can be worked for hours with
  little attention: safe enough not to punish a glance away, steady XP and a steady stack. It is slower per minute
  than an attentive dive (`skills-and-resources.md`), never pointless. "Brutal" lives in the deep, not at the rock
  you're mining by the burrow.
- **Every creature's loot stays worth something.** Farming easy creatures you've outgrown still pays: drops that
  feed crafting, cooking and building at every level, so a relaxed farming session feels good rather than wasted.
- **Building pulls you into everything else.** Bases, gear and the shared home need core and rare materials that
  come from different activities: gathering, fighting, diving, digging. No single loop supplies all of them, so a
  builder goes out into the world, and the world's activities all have a reason to exist.

## Pillars
| Pillar | Means | Feels like | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cozy | Home, food and friends are a warm refuge | Kelp stew in your shell-home after a dive | Cozy everywhere, so nothing is earned |
| Brutal | The deep is dangerous and indifferent, and bodies visibly break: hits crack shells and tear off limbs, on the crab and its enemies alike | Limping home with a cracked shell, a missing leg and a dimming lantern | Big damage numbers or punishing difficulty; cruel for its own sake, or punishing friends for playing together |
| Surreal | The real ocean is stranger than anything on land | A whale-fall village; an anglerfish that fills the sky at crab scale | Made up. If the real ocean doesn't do it, neither do we |

Every art, sound and design choice is checked against all three. A hat should be cozy to own, hard-won, and a bit
strange.

## Solo and co-op
- **Solo is the full game:** every zone, skill and story beat. Ironman-style: you can make everything yourself.
- **Co-op is optional,** 2–8 players, peer-to-peer in the browser (WebRTC via matchbox; the host's browser is the
  server, a tiny introducer, no database, cheating allowed among friends). Crisp for everyone: your own crab never
  feels the lag, and a parry is judged on your own screen. Each player keeps their crab in their own browser.
- A hosted shared world may come much later; refactor then rather than design for it now.

## The loop
Forage → Prepare (cook food, culture lantern bacteria) → Craft (tools and gear from shell, bone, glass, litter) →
Build (grow the shell-home, set rest stops) → Traverse (dive deeper, survive, come back). Then again, one zone deeper.

Touchstones: RuneScape, Hollow Knight / Silksong, Made in Abyss, Subnautica, Dredge, Don't Starve Together, Valheim.
Play Another Crab's Treasure so CrabScape ends up different, not similar.
