# Vision

A cozy-but-brutal adventure, solo or co-op: you are a small crab exploring a vast, strange ocean. Forage, cook,
craft and build your way deeper, and come home alive.

## The hook
**Feels like Valheim. Plays like Silksong. Grows like RuneScape. Underwater, and you're a crab.**

- **Feels like Valheim:** a wild world you set out into, alone or with friends, and a home you build yourself.
  Each region is a clear step up in danger and reward.
- **Plays like Silksong:** skilful movement and combat in a beautiful, hostile world that doesn't hold your hand.
- **Grows like RuneScape:** skills that grow by use, gathering and crafting; rarer things come from deeper places.
- **The twist:** every trip is a dive. Deeper means colder, darker, less oxygen, more pressure and stranger life,
  and you always have to plan the way back up.

## Pillars
| Pillar | Means | Feels like | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cozy | Home, food and friends are a warm refuge | Kelp stew in your shell-home after a dive | Cozy everywhere, so nothing is earned |
| Brutal | The deep is dangerous and indifferent, and bodies visibly break: hits crack shells and tear off limbs, on the crab and its enemies alike | Limping home with a cracked shell, a missing leg and a dimming lantern | Big damage numbers or punishing difficulty; cruel for its own sake, or punishing friends for playing together |
| Surreal | The real ocean is stranger than anything on land | A whale-fall village; an anglerfish that fills the sky at crab scale | Made up. If the real ocean doesn't do it, neither do we |

Every art, sound and design choice is checked against all three. A hat should be cozy to own, hard-won, and a bit
strange.

## Solo and co-op
- **Solo is the full game:** every zone, skill and story beat. Ironman-style: you can make everything yourself.
- **Co-op is optional,** 2–8 players, peer-to-peer in the browser (WebRTC via matchbox; the host's browser is the
  server, a tiny introducer, no database, cheating allowed among friends). Crisp for everyone: your own crab never
  feels the lag, and a parry is judged on your own screen. Each player keeps their crab in their own browser.
- A hosted shared world may come much later; refactor then rather than design for it now.

## The loop
Forage → Prepare (cook food, culture lantern bacteria) → Craft (tools and gear from shell, bone, glass, litter) →
Build (grow the shell-home, set rest stops) → Traverse (dive deeper, survive, come back). Then again, one zone deeper.

Touchstones: RuneScape, Hollow Knight / Silksong, Made in Abyss, Subnautica, Dredge, Don't Starve Together, Valheim.
Play Another Crab's Treasure so CrabScape ends up different, not similar.
