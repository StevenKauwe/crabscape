# Helping with CrabScape

You don't need to code, design games, or know anything about GitHub. Claude does the designing, building and all the
behind-the-scenes admin. What we need from you is **taste**: play the game, look at the art, and say honestly what
feels good and what feels off.

## Getting started (once)
1. Accept your invite: <https://github.com/StevenKauwe/crabscape/invitations>
2. Install Claude Code: <https://claude.com/claude-code>
3. Open Claude Code and say: "Set me up to work on CrabScape (StevenKauwe/crabscape)." Claude installs what it needs.
   If it asks you to type one line starting with `!` to sign in, do that and follow the prompts.

## Helping
Open Claude Code in the project and say what you'd like to do, or just type `/next-task`. Claude picks something that
fits your budget, does it, checks it and adds it to the game. If it runs out of budget halfway, that's fine: it
leaves a note so someone else's Claude can continue.

## Giving taste (the most useful thing you can do)
Use the Studio page: <https://claude.ai/artifact/PttxVTZCC75dqSEpKXYowf>. Say what you tried, what felt off, what
felt great. Short and honest is perfect. That's it.

## House rules, in plain words
- Everything in the game works like the real ocean. Crabs breathe water, so no air tanks.
- CrabScape is all rights reserved: you're welcome to look and help, but the code and art can't be reused elsewhere.
- Be kind. It's a game among friends.
