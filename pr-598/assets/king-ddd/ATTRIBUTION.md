# King Data Driven Decision source assets

The sample workbook comes from Steven Kauwe's existing `king-ddd-arena` project. The native region uses
CrabScape's existing articulated crab and painted body parts rather than the standalone arena's knights
and battlefield. The standalone original remains separately available at `/king-ddd/`. CrabScape's
all-rights-reserved policy applies to its original artwork and this sample.

## UI fonts

egui 0.36.2 embeds Hack Regular, Noto Emoji, Ubuntu Light and emoji-icon-font. These retain their own
licenses, not CrabScape's artwork terms. The original license texts ship in `font-licenses/`:
Hack/Bitstream Vera notices, SIL Open Font License 1.1, Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0 and the emoji-icon-font
MIT notice. Source: the `epaint_default_fonts` 0.36.2 crate and its upstream font projects.
